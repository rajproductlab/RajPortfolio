Product management skills

How I work as a PM, with the proof behind each skill.

Problem discovery & user research

Start from where users drop off and why, then validate with qualitative research instead of assuming.

Proof: Designed a user form and live call script to find why users stall at early levels; mapped onboarding friction at saasguru.

Data-driven diagnosis

Funnels, cohorts, and SQL to find the real cause, and to question whether the data itself is right.

Proof: Five-report funnel diagnostic that caught a merge-tag rendering bug and misleading windowed conversion ratios; per-user milestone view in Metabase.

Feature ideation & 0→1 product

Turn a behavioral insight into a feature with a clear success metric.

Proof: FBP carry-forward logic, Gobillion Coins rewards loop, and a subscription product that reached $45K incremental MRR.

Experimentation

Structured hypotheses, clean test design, and a standing habit of asking what the next experiment is.

Proof: 100+ A/B and multivariate tests across paywalls, onboarding, landing pages, and CRM journeys.

Strategy & roadmapping

Prioritize by impact, phase delivery, and design systems that scale without manual effort.

Proof: B2B-to-B2C growth automation blueprint with segments, journeys, dashboards, and a phased roadmap that limits RM involvement to exceptions.

Instrumentation & event design

If it isn’t tracked, it can’t be improved. I specify the events before the feature ships.

Proof: Event specs for the Advance Tax and Regime Audit flows; 15+ automated lifecycle triggers at Gobillion.

AI & automation product thinking

Use AI where it removes a manual bottleneck and turns raw data into a decision.

Proof: Callyzer call-intelligence pipeline and the MoEngage + Apps Script + Claude reporting automation.

Cross-functional leadership & GTM

Align engineering, design, sales, and analytics around one outcome and ship.

Proof: Led squads across engineering, design, content, and social against OKRs; GTM launch of a 0→1 subscription product.
